Stochastic Chess.

Moves

Rules

Normal chess rules, except your pieces don't always do what they're told. When you order a move, the piece weighs up where it's going:

  • Protected: another of your pieces guards the square. Least likely to disobey, even if the square is also threatened.
  • Unprotected: nothing guards it, but nothing attacks it either. More likely to disobey.
  • Threatened: an enemy attacks the square and nothing of yours guards it. Most likely to disobey.

A piece that disobeys gets scared. It moves to one of its own safer squares instead, if it has one. Otherwise it freezes and your turn is lost. While your king is in check, a piece with no safer square always obeys, so you're never left in check.

Tap a piece to see its moves. Each square is tinted by its risk, and the legend shows the odds. Change the odds in Settings.

The game is saved in this browser, so you can come back to it later.
