Probe.

Check-ins

    How it works

    Each need is a bar, like in The Sims: full and green is good, empty and red needs attention. Drag the sliders to how you feel right now. The diamond's colour shows your overall state.

    Save check-in records a snapshot, with an optional note. The small line under each need traces your last two weeks of check-ins, so patterns show up, like energy dipping every afternoon or mood tracking sleep.

    Share makes a link to your latest check-in, all 15 needs plus its note, to send to someone like a family member. Tapping it opens a read-only copy of your check-in on their phone. The values travel inside the link and aren't stored on any server, so anyone you send it to can see them. It's a snapshot: share again after your next check-in to update them.

    Beyond the classic needs, Probe adds Thirst, plus reflection gauges: Mood, Calm (stress), Focus, Movement, Outdoors (daylight and fresh air), and Purpose (whether today feels meaningful). Hide any you don't want with Choose needs.

    Your data stays in this browser. Export data saves one ZIP with spreadsheet-ready CSV files, a readable summary (README.md) and a full backup. Download backup saves just the backup, which Restore can load again.
