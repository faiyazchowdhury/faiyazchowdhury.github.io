Chords.

    How points work

    Progressions are written as scale degrees, so they hold in any key: upper case is a major chord, lower case is minor, and b lowers the root a semitone. For example, I V vi IV is C G Am F in C, or G D Em C in G.

    A progression's points are the number of songs you know that use it. The more songs you can play over a progression, the deeper it's in your hands.
