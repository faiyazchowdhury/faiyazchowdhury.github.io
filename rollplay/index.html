    How the die works

    Each activity has a weight: how much of your time you'd like it to get, relative to the others. With nothing logged, an activity with weight 6 comes up twice as often as one with weight 3.

    The die also looks at the last 7 days. It works out each activity's fair share of what you've logged (its weight's share of the total) and compares it with what you actually did. The chance is weight × (1 + fair share) ÷ (1 + times done). Something you've neglected climbs, and something you've overdone drops, until things even out.

    Tap Done after a roll, or the + next to any activity, to log it. Tap an activity's name to rename or delete it.
