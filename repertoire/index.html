Repertoire.

    How it works

    Learned is how much of the piece you can play, from 0 to 100%.

    Performed goes up each time you tap + after playing the piece through for someone, or for a recording. It grows logarithmically: the first few performances count the most, and each doubling of the count adds the same amount. Ten performances fill the bar about halfway, and a hundred fill it.
