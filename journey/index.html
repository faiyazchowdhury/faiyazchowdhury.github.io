Journey.

    How it works

    Each goal breaks into steps, and its ring fills as you tick them off. The first unticked step shows on the card as Next, so you always know what to do. A goal with no steps uses a progress slider instead.

    Add notes along the way, such as what you tried, what you learned or how it felt, and they're dated automatically. Tap Edit to change a goal's title, its reason or its target year.

    The starting steps are suggestions. Rename, delete or add your own.
